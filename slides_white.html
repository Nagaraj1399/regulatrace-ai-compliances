REGULATRACE AI Problem Statement
02 / 10

The Banking Compliance Crisis

Tier-1 financial institutions spend billions annually manually reconciling thousands of complex regulatory obligations across DORA, OCC 2011-12, Basel III, and MAS guidelines.

⚠️ Fragmented & Manual Spreadsheets

Compliance teams spend 65%+ of their hours cross-referencing multi-hundred-page policy PDFs against disconnected internal control matrices in Excel.

  • Extremely slow, error-prone manual reviews
  • Massive analyst burnout and high turnover
  • Lag of weeks before new circulars are mapped
❌ Black-Box AI Hallucination Risk

Generic LLMs hallucinate control adequacy, skip subtle sub-clauses, and lack deterministic explainability required by regulatory bank examiners.

  • Unsubstantiated control approval risks fines
  • No exact paragraph and sub-clause citations
  • Regulators reject unexplainable black-box AI
⚖️ Deficient Audit Trails & Governance

Auditors from FED, ECB, PRA, or RBI demand tamper-evident proof of who approved, modified, or identified gaps for every mandatory obligation.

  • Informal or missing analyst rationales
  • Untracked remediation plans for partial gaps
  • Zero end-to-end cryptographic traceability

REGULATRACE AI Proposed Solution
03 / 10

RegulaTrace AI: Autonomous Compliance Workbench

An end-to-end Agentic Compliance Platform purpose-built for Tier-1 banks, combining autonomous AI intelligence with strict human-in-the-loop governance.

✨ Three-Pillar Agentic Workflow
  • Agent 1: Zero-Shot Clause Ingestion Engine: Ingests complex circular text (DORA ICT, OCC 2011-12, Basel III) and extracts atomic, traceable obligations with exact citations.
  • Agent 2: Multi-Factor Semantic Matching Engine: Evaluates internal bank controls against extracted obligations using a 4-dimensional mathematical scoring formula.
  • Agent 3: Remediation & Gap Advisor: Identifies unaddressed sub-requirements and generates actionable remediation guidance for compliance officers.
🛡️ Strict Human-In-The-Loop Governance

AI proposes; human experts adjudicate. RegulaTrace AI enforces enterprise safeguards before any mapping is finalized:

  • Mandatory Rationale Validation: Enforces minimum 10-character substantive justification for every approval.
  • Enforced Gap Acknowledgment: Partial/gap matches require explicit sign-off on remediation plans.
  • Tamper-Evident Audit Ledger: Every decision, RFI, and role change is permanently recorded with timestamps and user identities.

End-to-End Enterprise Architecture

A high-throughput, modular architecture linking regulatory sources, AI inference, deterministic scoring, and audit immutability.

LAYER 1: INGESTION
Document Parser

Parses PDFs, circulars, and regulatory updates across jurisdictions (EU, US, UK, MAS). Extracts section excerpts and metadata.

LAYER 2: AI REASONING
Gemini Agent

Google Gemini 1.5 Flash extracts atomic obligations, splits sub-components, and drafts expert match rationales in structured JSON.

LAYER 3: MATCHING MATH
4-Factor Match Engine

Deterministic calculation combining semantic similarity, token overlap, business function alignment, and control effectiveness.

LAYER 4: GOVERNANCE
Audit Ledger & 3LoD

Interactive 3-column workbench with role-switching (1st, 2nd, 3rd Line of Defense), RFI workflows, and board-ready export.

🔄 Closed-Loop Traceability Pipeline

Raw Circular Text ➔ Clause Tokenization ➔ Structured Obligation ➔ Candidate Control Scoring (High / Medium / Low) ➔ Analyst Adjudication Barrier ➔ Immutable Audit History ➔ Real-Time Coverage Matrix

REGULATRACE AI Mathematical Rigor
07 / 10

Explainable 4-Factor AI Confidence Scoring

Transparent scoring mathematics that bankers and regulatory examiners can inspect, calibrate, and verify.

WEIGHT: 35%
Semantic Cosine

Cosine similarity between obligation requirement embeddings and control descriptions to capture regulatory intent.

WEIGHT: 25%
Lexical Overlap

Token-level Jaccard index matching regulatory jargon (e.g., 'failover', 'RTO', 'multi-factor authentication').

WEIGHT: 20%
Domain Alignment

Exact match between banking functions (e.g., IT Operations, Treasury & AML, Model Governance, Risk Management).

WEIGHT: 20%
Control Robustness

Evaluates internal control frequency (automated vs manual), test history, and operating effectiveness.

📐 Composite Confidence Score Formula

Score = (0.35 × Sim_Semantic) + (0.25 × Sim_Lexical) + (0.20 × Score_Domain) + (0.20 × Score_Robustness)
High (≥ 80%): Automated Mapping Recommended | Medium (60–79%): Analyst Review Required | Low (< 60%): Potential Control Gap Flagged
